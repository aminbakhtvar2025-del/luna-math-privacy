Privacy policy

Luna Math AI ("the app") is a math app for children and teenagers aged 7 to 17. We built it so that nothing about your child leaves the phone.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no names or e-mail addresses.
  • No ads and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No chat, no sharing, no contact with other users.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts.
  • We (the developer) receive no data from the app at all. We have no server for it.

What stays on the phone

Your child's age band, chosen language, progress, stars and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Google services used on the phone

  • Handwriting (Google ML Kit Digital Ink). When your child first uses the writing pad, the phone downloads a handwriting model from Google (about 20 MB). The writing itself is recognised on the phone and is not sent anywhere. Google's ML Kit may send Google anonymous technical information (for example device type, app version and performance), as described in Google's ML Kit terms: developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms.
  • AI hints (Gemini Nano, on supported phones). On phones with Google's on-device AI, the app can ask Gemini Nano to word a hint for the current problem. The request contains only the math problem, never anything your child typed or wrote, and it is processed on the phone by Android's AICore. Grown-ups can switch AI hints off and hide any hint.
  • Voice packs (Google Play). Luna's voice in each language is delivered by Google Play as part of the app.

Children

The app is designed for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and it complies with Google Play's Families policy.
